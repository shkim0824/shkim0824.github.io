About

I am a master’s student at KAIST, advised by Prof. Juho Lee, and currently an intern at Kakao. My research focuses on model consolidation for large language models.

I explore model merging and knowledge distillation to integrate knowledge and complementary capabilities from multiple models into a unified model. My goal is to preserve these capabilities across diverse tasks and objectives.
