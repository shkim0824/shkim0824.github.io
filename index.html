About

I am a master’s student at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Juho Lee, and currently an intern at Kakao. My research focuses on model consolidation for large language models.

I explore model merging and knowledge distillation to combine knowledge and complementary capabilities learned by different models. This includes approaches such as multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD), with the broader goal of building unified models that perform well across diverse tasks and objectives.
